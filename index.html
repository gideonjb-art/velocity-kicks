Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary by location — Nairobi and its environs enjoy same-day delivery. Other regions: 2-4 business days with real-time tracking.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, and bank transfers. All payments are encrypted and secure.

Can I return a product?

Yes. Returns are accepted within 7 days, provided the product is unworn, with original packaging and tags.

How do I track my order?

Once shipped, you’ll receive SMS/Email tracking link. You can also chat with our support via WhatsApp.

Are your sneakers authentic?

100% authentic guarantee. Every pair is sourced directly from verified distributors and hand-inspected by our team.
